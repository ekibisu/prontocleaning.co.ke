Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

ProntoCleaning Solutions ("ProntoCleaning," "we," "us," or "our") provides residential and commercial cleaning services in Nairobi, Kenya. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to www.prontocleaning.co.ke and does not cover third-party sites we may link to.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels on this site.

Quote and contact requests. When you request a quote or get in touch, we collect what you enter into the form — typically your name, email address, phone number, property address, property details (type, bedrooms, bathrooms, size), preferred date, cleaning type, and any notes or photos you choose to attach.

Newsletter sign-up. If you subscribe for cleaning tips and offers, we collect the email address you provide.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs standard technical data — such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps — for security and reliability purposes. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request and provide an accurate estimate
  • To schedule, deliver, and follow up on cleaning services
  • To send the newsletter, if you've opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To respond to questions sent through our contact channels
  • To keep our website secure and working properly

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the above without asking you first.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with the service providers that help us run this website and respond to your requests:

  • Resend — delivers quote and contact form submissions to our team by email.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and provides security and performance infrastructure.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ProntoCleaning Solutions, our clients, or others.

4. Data Retention

We keep quote requests and related correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Newsletter email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

5. Your Rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and why
  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal or legitimate business retention needs
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including unsubscribing from marketing emails at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll respond as promptly as we can.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including transmitting this site over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your data safe and limit access to what our team and service providers need to do their jobs.

7. Photos You Send Us

If you attach photos to a quote request (for example, to help us scope a job), we use them only to prepare your estimate and deliver the related service. We don't publish client-submitted photos without separate, explicit permission.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services and this website are intended for adults arranging cleaning services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or applicable law change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here.
